2. Noun endings in the Genitiv

GenderNominativGenitivRule
maskulinder Manndes Mannes+ -es (or -s)
maskulinder Tagdes Tagesone-syllable → + -es
neutraldas Kinddes Kindes+ -es
feminindie Frauder Frauno noun ending change
Pluraldie Kinderder Kinderno noun ending change

Masculine and neuter nouns ending in -s, -ß, -z, -x, and most one-syllable nouns, take -es rather than just -s: das Glas → des Glases, der Fluss → des Flusses. Feminine and plural nouns never change — only the article shows the case.

4. Register: Genitiv vs. von + Dativ

Formal / written (Genitiv)Colloquial / spoken (von + Dativ)
das Auto meines Vatersdas Auto von meinem Vater
die Farbe des Himmelsdie Farbe von dem (vom) Himmel

The Genitiv expresses possession/relationship mainly in formal and written German. In everyday speech, von + Dativ is much more common.
